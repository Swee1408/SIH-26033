## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Farm2Fork is an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, wholesalers, and bulk buyers through a single platform.

The main objective is to eliminate unnecessary intermediaries, increase farmers' income, reduce consumer prices, and improve the efficiency of the agricultural supply chain.

Key Features
Direct Marketplace: Farmers can sell their agricultural products directly to consumers and bulk buyers without depending on multiple middlemen.

AI-Based Price Prediction: AI analyzes market prices, product demand, seasonal changes, and location to recommend fair selling prices.

Demand Forecasting: Machine learning predicts future demand and helps farmers make better production and selling decisions.

Smart Farmer Aggregation: Combines agricultural products from multiple nearby farmers to fulfill large orders.

Smart Logistics: Connects farmers with transport providers and identifies cost-effective delivery routes.

Real-Time Tracking: Buyers and farmers can track orders and delivery status.

Digital Payments: Provides secure online payments and transparent transaction records.

Multilingual Support: Supports regional languages to make the platform easy to use for rural farmers.

Innovation: An intelligent system that automatically identifies nearby farmers, combines their available produce to fulfill bulk orders, recommends fair prices, and plans efficient delivery routes.

Methodology and Implementation
Step 1 – User Registration: Farmers, FPOs, consumers, retailers, and transport providers register on the platform.

Step 2 – Product Listing: Farmers upload product names, available quantities, quality details, expected prices, and locations.

Step 3 – AI Price Recommendation: The system analyzes market trends and demand to suggest suitable selling prices.

Step 4 – Buyer Connection: Consumers and bulk buyers search for products, compare prices, and place orders directly.

Step 5 – Smart Farmer Aggregation: When a bulk order is placed, the system identifies multiple nearby farmers who can collectively supply the required quantity.

Step 6 – Logistics Optimization: The platform calculates efficient transportation routes based on distance, vehicle capacity, delivery locations, and transportation costs.

Step 7 – Order Completion: Products are delivered, payments are processed, and transaction details are maintained digitally.

## Feasibility and Viability
Analysis of Feasibility
The proposed solution is technically, economically, and operationally feasible because it uses existing technologies, open-source software, and affordable cloud infrastructure.

Technical Feasibility: AI frameworks, mobile application technologies, and mapping APIs can support the development.

Economic Feasibility: Cloud-based infrastructure reduces initial investment and maintenance costs.

Operational Feasibility: Farmers can access the platform using smartphones with simple regional language interfaces.

Scalability: The platform can initially be launched in selected villages or districts and gradually expanded across India.

Potential Challenges and Risks
Limited internet connectivity in rural areas.

Lack of digital knowledge among farmers.

Transportation and delivery difficulties.

Differences in product quality and quantity.

Fluctuating agricultural market prices.

Trust and payment-related issues.

Strategies for Overcoming Challenges
Develop a simple and multilingual mobile application.

Provide voice-based assistance for farmers.

Enable offline product listing with automatic synchronization.

Introduce verified farmer and buyer accounts.

Partner with local transport providers and FPOs.

Implement product quality verification.

Maintain transparent pricing and secure digital transactions.
